06 — Frequently Asked Questions

Legal & Financial FAQs
Are the properties listed on R4Realty RERA-approved?

Yes. R4Realty only lists properties that are verified and registered with their respective state portals (Uttar Pradesh RERA or Goa RERA). The individual RERA numbers (such as UPRERAPRJ251909 for GYGY Mentis) are listed transparently on each project page.

Do I need to pay brokerage fees to R4Realty?

No. R4Realty works directly under builder developer authorizations (Sikka Group, Bhutani, Ebrix Developers). We guarantee direct developer inventory allocation and pre-launch prices with zero brokerage charges for buyers.

How does the pre-leased monthly return guarantee work?

In commercial projects like Sikka Mall of Expressway and GYGY Mentis, developers offer a pre-possession monthly assured return (e.g. 1% monthly or 2% pre-leased returns) to secure cash flow for investors. Post-possession, the units are leased to retail chains or brand outlets with long-term lock-in lease agreements.

What are the plot configurations in Vedic City Goa?

Vedic City North Goa offers three premium plot configurations: Graama (RERA villa plots starting at 300 gaj), Anandam (pre-launch residential plots starting at 240 gaj), and Praana (exclusive gated farmhouse plots starting at 1380 gaj). All configurations feature RERA approvals and registry.
